Directions

  1. Tape a rectangle on the fridge (painter’s tape) — or use the goal posts & crossbar.
  2. Tap 📷 Camera below and prop the phone 6–10 ft back, facing the target.
  3. Drag the red corner dots onto the corners of your rectangle.
  4. Want to call your shot? Tap a zone in Call your shot below first.
  5. Hit ▶ Start tracking and fire when you see the green ● READY.
  6. After each shot it pauses ~7 seconds while you grab the beanbag, then says “Ready!” out loud.

Two iPads: this one on Camera pointed at the target, the other one open on Game screen with the same room code — it shows READY, calls the shots, and lets J pick target zones from across the room.

Tips for the fridge trial

• Tape a rectangle on the fridge (painter’s tape) and drag the corners to match.
• Beanbags, rolled socks, tape balls — anything that contrasts with the fridge works.
• Phone on a chair or counter, 6–10 ft back, steady. Good light helps a lot.
• Too many phantom hits? Lower sensitivity. Missing real ones? Raise it.
• At home: same thing, corners on the goal posts & crossbar. Foam pucks first!
