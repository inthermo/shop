The shape of the week

Two homes for the price of one week

The house, Friday to Friday

A detached holiday house in Gaastmeer, a tiny village on the Fluessen and Gaastmeer lakes in the south-west corner of Friesland. Three bedrooms, sleeps eight, a south-facing terrace on the water and a private jetty at the bottom of the garden. This is base camp all week, and home every night for whoever is not sleeping aboard.

The boat, Monday to Friday

A lemsteraak, the classic Frisian flat-bottomed sailing boat with wooden leeboards and red-brown "tanbark" sails. Ten metres long, seven berths, a proper galley and a loo below. No licence is needed on these lakes, and the boat is ours without a skipper. It gets delivered to our jetty on Monday afternoon and goes back Friday morning.

For the sailing crew

Three nights, four villages

Short hops of two to four hours a day on sheltered lakes. Bridges open for you, speed limits keep everyone honest, and every evening ends in a village harbour with a bakery at the end of the dock. The sailing party is two adults and the four kids; the boat has room for all six.

  • Night 1
    Heeg

    Across the Heegermeer to the old watersport village. Harbour cafés, ice cream on the quay.

  • Night 2
    Woudsend

    Quiet canal village between two lakes, with the Slotermeer right next door for an afternoon of open water.

  • Night 3
    Sloten or Sneek

    Sloten is the smallest of Friesland's eleven cities, all canals and windmills. Sneek is bigger, with the Waterpoort gate and shops. Pick by mood.

  • Stretch
    Stavoren

    Through the Johan Frisokanaal to the IJsselmeer harbour and a beach. Only in settled weather and with an early start.

For the shore party

Spa days while the crew is out

Friesland does the Dutch sauna culture properly: thermal pools, a sauna garden, hamam rituals, and a massage and facial menu. The closest and best is about half an hour from the house, so a spa day fits easily between breakfast on the terrace and dinner with the returning sailors.

Joure · about 30 min · first choice

De Woudfennen

The region's big wellness resort. Finnish, herbal, salt-stone, Turkish steam and a panoramic kota sauna, a hamam ritual, indoor and outdoor pools, massages, and a beauty institute for facials and combination treatments. There is a hotel on site if a night away appeals.

Details
Bergum · about 45 min

Sauna de Leeuwerikhoeve

A farmhouse sauna in the Frisian woods. Finnish and salt-stone saunas, herbal bath, hammam, massages, body and facial treatments, and a restaurant for a long lunch.

Details
Vrouwenparochie · about 50 min

De Zoutbron

Saunas plus a warm salt-water bath fed by its own underground spring. Massages, and a bed and breakfast attached. Smaller and calmer than the big resorts.

Minnertsga · about 45 min

Wellness Instituut Lucia

Friesland's first hammam, with sauna, whirlpool and a menu of scrubs and detox treatments. Good for a half day with the traditional soap-and-steam ritual.

Details

Good to know: most Dutch saunas are swimwear-free by default, and the bigger ones run one or two swimwear days a week. Check the calendar when booking treatments, and book massages ahead for July.

For everyone

Day trips from the front door

Nearly everything is within half an hour. The eleven old Frisian cities are tiny, pretty and close together, the coast is twenty minutes west, and the bikes-and-boats network means the kids can get around on their own.

Hindeloopen · 15 min

Painted town on the dike

Hand-painted furniture, a harbour behind the sea wall, and the ice-skating museum that tells the story of the Elfstedentocht, the eleven-cities skating race.

Workum · 15 min

Jopie Huisman Museum

A rag-and-bone man who painted worn-out shoes and old coats with astonishing tenderness. Open daily 11 to 5 in summer. Pair it with coffee on the square.

Sloten · 15 min

The smallest city

A fortified town the size of a village: one canal, one windmill, lime trees along the water. Reachable by boat as well as by car.

Stavoren · 20 min

Beach and ferry

IJsselmeer harbour with a sandy beach at Vrouwezand. A foot-and-bike ferry crosses to Enkhuizen across the water.

Lemmer · 25 min

Woudagemaal

The largest working steam pumping station in the world, a UNESCO site that still fires up when the water gets too high. Open Tuesday to Sunday.

Sneek · 25 min

Waterpoort and the maritime museum

The town gate on the water is the postcard of Friesland. The Fries Scheepvaart Museum covers the boats we will be sailing. Best shopping in the area.

Makkum and the Afsluitdijk · 40 min

Beach and the big dike

Wide sandy beach at Makkum, then a drive out onto the 32 km dike that closed off the sea in 1932, with the Wadden on one side and the IJsselmeer on the other.

Harlingen · 45 min

Ferry to the islands

Fast ferries to Terschelling and Vlieland, car-free Wadden islands with dune beaches. A long day, or an overnight for the shore party.

Leeuwarden · 45 min

The capital

City day with a leaning tower, canals, Aqua Zoo and the Natuurmuseum Fryslân for the kids.

Oudemirdum · 10 min

Sybrandy's Speelpark

Old-fashioned family play park in the woods behind the Gaasterland hills, ten minutes from the house. The easy answer on a cloudy morning.

On two wheels

Elfstedenroute by bike

Flat, signed cycle routes link all eleven cities. The numbered junction network makes a half-day loop simple to plan from the house.

Giethoorn · 1 h 15

The village without roads

Thatched farmhouses on canals, punts instead of cars. Far enough that it deserves its own day, probably with everyone ashore.

Getting there

Amsterdam to the jetty

Fly into Amsterdam Schiphol. Gaastmeer is about an hour and twenty minutes north by car along the A6 and A7, or two hours by train and taxi via Leeuwarden. Pick up the house keys Friday from 4 pm.

Who sleeps where

Room for all eight

Three bedrooms in the house, seven berths on the boat. Friday to Monday everyone is in the house. Monday to Thursday night the sailing crew of six sleeps aboard in whichever harbour they reach, and the two of you have the house, the terrace and the spa to yourselves.
